About this methods bank
This website is an open repository of child-computer interaction research methods developed by the INTERACT Research Unit at the University of Oulu. Materials are freely available to teachers, researchers, and practitioners.
What is this methods bank?
The INTERACT CCI Research Methods Bank is a growing, open platform for sharing research-backed child-computer interaction methods with the people who need them most — teachers, researchers, social workers, and other practitioners working with children and technology.
The bank currently contains materials from two INTERACT projects: the MAD project (Make-A-Difference, 2020–2023) and the Takeover project (ongoing). More projects will be added as INTERACT research continues.
All materials are freely available under an open licence. No registration is required to browse or download. This website was developed as part of a traineeship project by Ritu Basak (MSc student, University of Oulu) under the supervision of Prof. Tonja Molin-Juustila and Prof. Netta Iivari.
About INTERACT
INTERACT is a research unit at the University of Oulu focused on Human-Computer Interaction, Participatory Design, and user-centred technology. The group studies how people — especially children — can actively shape and design the technology around them.
