Technology friendship book
An individual written exercise in which children explore and reflect on their personal relationship with technology. Children personify a chosen piece of technology as a 'friend' and describe this relationship using guided questions in a workbook format.
The exercise forms the opening activity of Phase 1 — Sensitizing with Technology — and helps children articulate feelings about technology they may not have previously put into words. It can be used with children aged 7–15 and requires no prior preparation from the teacher beyond printing the worksheet.
1
Introduce the concept of personifying technology as a friend. Ask children to choose one piece of technology they use regularly.
2
Distribute the Technology Friendship Book worksheet. Allow 20 minutes for children to complete it individually and quietly.
3
Invite volunteers to share their descriptions. Facilitate a short class discussion about different relationships with technology.
